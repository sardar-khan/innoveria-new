The AI agent for healthcare that keeps phones answered and schedules full

An AI agent for healthcare answers your phones 24/7, books and reschedules appointments, sends reminders patients confirm with one tap, and completes intake before the visit, so no shows drop and your front desk stops drowning. Anything clinical routes straight to your staff, and HIPAA is designed in from day one.

The leaks we see in every practice

Where clinic revenue quietly disappears

Phones ringing past a busy front desk, no shows that leave providers paid to wait, and intake paperwork retyped into the EHR by hand. Every one of them is automatable.

One front desk, two jobs at once

Your team cannot answer the phone and check in the patient standing at the counter at the same time. Callers who hit voicemail book with the practice that picked up.

No shows leave providers paid to wait

Every empty slot is a provider on the clock with nobody in the room. A single voicemail reminder two days out does not move the number, and the waitlist never hears about the opening.

Intake typed twice, checked never

Patients fill out clipboards in the waiting room, then your staff retypes every field into the EHR. Slow for patients, error prone for the chart, and a daily hour your team never gets back.

The system we build for you

A front desk that never puts anyone on hold

One connected system that answers every call, confirms every appointment, backfills every cancellation, and completes intake before the patient arrives. Clinical questions always route to staff.

24/7 AI receptionist

Patients call to book, reschedule, or cancel and get it done on the first ring, at lunch, at midnight, on weekends. The schedule updates in real time and your front desk starts the day with clean books instead of a full voicemail box.

Two touch reminders, one tap confirm

A reminder days out and another the day before, by text or WhatsApp, each with a one tap confirm or reschedule. Patients who need to move get a new slot in the same thread instead of ghosting the appointment.

Automatic waitlist backfill

The moment a slot opens, the system offers it to matching patients on your waitlist and books the first to accept. Cancellations stop meaning empty rooms and start meaning shorter waits.

Digital intake before the visit

Patients complete forms on their phone before they arrive and the data lands where it belongs instead of being retyped from a clipboard. Anything clinical or sensitive routes to your staff, never to the machine.

Questions practices ask

Healthcare automation, answered directly

What is an AI agent for healthcare?

An AI agent for healthcare is software that handles the front desk work that needs no clinical judgment: answering calls, booking and rescheduling appointments, sending reminders, backfilling from the waitlist, and collecting intake before the visit. Anything clinical or sensitive routes straight to your staff, so patients always reach a human when it matters.

Is an AI agent for healthcare HIPAA compliant?

Yes, when it is built the way we build it. Every vendor in the chain signs a BAA, data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access controls restrict who can see what. PHI routes to your staff through secured channels and is never handled in the open. You review the full vendor chain before anything goes live.

How much can it cut no shows?

Practices that move from a single reminder to two touch reminders with one tap confirmation, plus automatic waitlist backfill for the slots that still open up, typically cut no shows by 25 to 38 percent. The anonymized outpatient clinic on this page landed at 31 percent within three months of go live.

What does an AI agent for healthcare cost?

A complete front desk system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees, depending on call volume and how many channels you connect. Most clinics recover the build cost from filled no show slots within the first months. You get a fixed quote after a free audit.

Will it replace our front desk staff?

No. It takes the phone tag, the reminder calls, and the retyping off their plate. Your front desk greets the patients standing in front of them, handles the sensitive conversations, and reviews exceptions, instead of choosing between the ringing phone and the check in line.
