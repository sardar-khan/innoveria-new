Questions agents ask

Real estate automation, answered directly

Why does lead response speed matter so much in real estate?

Because most buyers work with the first agent who responds. Contact odds collapse within minutes of an enquiry arriving, and portal leads arrive heaviest in the evening when agents are showing homes or off the clock. Automation answers in under 60 seconds at any hour, so you win the race without being awake for it.

Does this work with Follow Up Boss, kvCORE, or Real Geeks?

Yes. We build on top of the CRM you already run rather than replacing it. Leads land as proper records, every automated touch gets logged, and stages update when viewings get booked. If your CRM has an API, we can connect it.

Is automated lead handling fair housing compliant?

It must be, and ours is designed for it. Qualification and routing logic never uses protected characteristics, and every message template goes through your review before go live. Automated scoring can produce discriminatory effects even without intent, so we keep protected attributes out of the logic entirely.

What does real estate automation cost?

A complete lead response system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees. Against one saved commission it usually pays back within the first month or two. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your lead flow.

Will this replace my ISA or my agents?

No. Automation takes the first touch, the qualification questions, and the reminders. Your ISA and agents get leads that already answered timeline, budget, and financing questions, with full context attached, and spend their time on conversations that close.
