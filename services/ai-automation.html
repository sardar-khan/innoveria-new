Questions

AI automation services, answered directly

What are AI automation services?

AI automation services design and build systems that handle repetitive business work automatically: answering leads, taking calls, sending follow ups, moving data between tools, and posting content. You describe the outcome, the provider builds the system, and your team keeps the hours it used to spend on admin.

How much do AI automation services cost?

A single workflow typically starts in the low hundreds of dollars, and a complete system such as full lead response usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. We quote a fixed price after a free audit, so there are no open ended bills.

How long does an AI automation project take?

One to two weeks for a single workflow, two to four weeks for a complete system including testing against your real data. You see the automation working before you pay the final invoice.

Do we need to replace our current software?

Almost never. We build on top of the CRM, calendar, phone system, and inbox you already use. If a tool has an API, we can connect it. Replacing software is the last resort, not the starting point.

Who owns the automation after the project?

You do. Every workflow runs in accounts registered to your business, and every build ships with documentation. If you stop working with us tomorrow, everything keeps running and any competent operator can maintain it.

Will automation replace our staff?

No. Automation takes the repetitive, time sensitive work: first replies, reminders, data entry, routing. Your team keeps the judgment work and gains back 20 or more hours a week to spend on clients instead of admin.
