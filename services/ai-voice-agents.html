Questions

AI voice agent services, answered directly

What are AI voice agent services?

AI voice agent services build a phone agent that answers your business line on the first ring, any hour of any day. It holds a natural conversation, answers questions about your business, books appointments straight into your calendar, and transfers calls that need a human along with a written summary. Callers who hang up early get an instant text back.

Does an AI voice agent sound robotic?

Modern voice agents speak in natural, current generation voices, handle interruptions, and follow the caller's lead instead of forcing a menu. We tune the greeting, tone, and vocabulary to your business, and you listen to test calls and approve the voice before it ever answers a real customer.

What happens when a call is too complex for the agent?

The agent transfers it. It recognizes when a caller needs judgment, is upset, or asks for a person, then routes the call to the right team member with a summary of everything said so far. Outside business hours it takes a detailed message, books a callback, and sends your team the transcript.

How much do AI voice agent services cost?

A complete voice agent, including call flows, calendar booking, transfers, and missed call text back, usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform and call fees. We quote one fixed price after a free audit, so there are no open ended bills.

How long does setup take?

One to two weeks for a straightforward answering and booking agent, two to four weeks for a full system with transfers, CRM sync, and missed call recovery. You listen to the agent handle real test calls before you pay the final invoice.

What about compliance and call recording consent?

We configure the agent for your jurisdiction: recording disclosures in the greeting where consent is required, data retention rules you control, and HIPAA compliant platforms for healthcare. Recordings and transcripts live in accounts your business owns, and you decide what is kept and for how long.
