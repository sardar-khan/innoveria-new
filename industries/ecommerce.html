The leaks we see in every store

Where store revenue quietly disappears

Abandoned carts chased with generic emails, a support desk buried in order status tickets, and social channels that go silent exactly when traffic peaks. Every one of them is automatable.

Seven of ten carts walk away

Most shoppers load a cart and leave, and the generic recovery email lands in promotions and gets ignored. That is real revenue sitting one good conversation away from checkout.

The inbox is a WISMO queue

Where is my order tickets bury your support desk, and every one of them is a copy paste of a tracking link your system already knows. Real problems wait behind them.

Social goes quiet every peak season

When Black Friday traffic spikes, DMs and comments pile up unanswered because everyone is packing orders. The quarter that should build your audience is the quarter you ghost it.

The system we build for you

Agents that sell, answer, and update while you sleep

One connected system that recovers carts conversationally, answers support from live order data, updates customers before they ask, and keeps social moving through peak season.

Conversational cart recovery

Triggered by real shopper behavior, not a timer. The message references the actual cart, answers objections about sizing or shipping, and carries the shopper back to checkout by email or WhatsApp. It stops the moment they buy.

Support that knows the order

A chatbot trained on your policies and connected to live order data answers WISMO, returns, and exchange questions in seconds, 24/7. Anything unusual hands off to a human with the full conversation attached.

Shipping updates before they ask

Order confirmed, shipped, out for delivery, delayed: customers hear it from you first, on the channel they prefer. Proactive updates are how the WISMO queue empties for good.

Social that survives Q4

Comments answered, DMs triaged, product questions handled from your catalog, and launches scheduled across channels. Peak season stops meaning silent socials.

Questions store owners ask

Ecommerce automation, answered directly

What are AI agents for ecommerce?

AI agents for ecommerce are software workers that handle the repetitive revenue and support work around your store: recovering abandoned carts with real conversations, answering order status and policy questions from live data, sending proactive shipping updates, and keeping social channels answered through peak season. Anything unusual hands off to your team.

Does it integrate with Shopify?

Yes. Shopify is the center of most builds we do. The agents read live order, inventory, and customer data through the API, trigger on real shopper behavior, and write everything back so your store stays the single source of truth. Klaviyo, your 3PL, and your support desk connect the same way.

How much revenue can cart recovery add?

It depends on your traffic, AOV, and how many carts you currently lose. One DTC brand we built for recovers about $11k in monthly revenue from conversational recovery across email and WhatsApp. Your number scales with your abandoned cart volume, and we project it from your actual store data in the free audit rather than promising a percentage.

What do AI agents for ecommerce cost?

A complete build typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees, depending on how many channels and systems we connect. Recovered carts and deflected tickets usually cover the build within the first months. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your store.

Will the bot annoy my customers?

Not if it is built with restraint. Recovery messages trigger on real behavior, reference the actual cart, and stop the moment someone buys or opts out. The support agent answers from your real policies and order data, and hands off to a human the moment a conversation needs one. Every template passes your review before go live.
