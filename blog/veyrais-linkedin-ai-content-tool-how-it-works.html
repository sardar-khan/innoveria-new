Veyrais: the LinkedIn AI content tool we built for ourselves, now open to everyone

Published August 25, 2026 · by Ahsan Ilyas, Founder of Innovaria Tech · 9 min read

Every post from Innovaria Tech that you have ever seen on LinkedIn went through the same system. We did not build it to sell it. We built it because writing a good post every morning does not survive contact with a busy week, and we got tired of watching consistency lose to whatever felt urgent that day. It worked well enough, for long enough, that we opened it up. That system is Veyrais.

Quick answer: Veyrais is an AI content tool built specifically for LinkedIn. It learns your actual voice, sources real trends from Reddit, Medium, Google, and LinkedIn itself, generates a batch of posts in one sitting, schedules them across the days and times you choose, and includes a review step before anything goes live. It also generates images and converts posts directly into carousels, one of the highest engagement formats on LinkedIn. Plans start at fifteen dollars a month for Starter, twenty five dollars a month for Growth, and forty dollars a month for Ultimate. You can see the actual product, not a mockup, at veyrais.innovariatech.space.

Want to see it before you commit to anything? The full breakdown of how we built and still run it ourselves is on our Veyrais case study page.

  • Veyrais was built and proven on our own LinkedIn presence before it was ever offered to anyone else, every post we publish still runs through it today.
  • It handles the mechanical parts of a consistent presence, drafting, trend sourcing, and scheduling, while a review step keeps a person in control of what actually goes live.

Why we built it in the first place

An agency's LinkedIn presence brings in real clients. Everyone in this business knows that. What nobody talks about honestly is how hard it is to actually sustain, not because writing one good post is difficult, but because writing one every single day, indefinitely, while running an actual business, is a different problem entirely. The important thing loses to the urgent thing, over and over, and a LinkedIn presence goes quiet not from a bad decision but from a hundred small ones.

We treated our own bottleneck the way we treat a client's. We audited it, found exactly where the leak was, writing itself was not the hard part, sustaining it was, and built a system around that specific problem. An AI ghostwriter tuned to our actual voice and niches. Trend sourcing pulled from where real conversations happen, not a generic topic list. Batch generation so a month of content gets built in one sitting instead of daily. A review step, because publishing on autopilot without a check is how a tool embarrasses you instead of helping you.

What it actually does

It learns a real voice, not a generic one. You set your background, audience, and niches, and choose from ten tone styles. Every post gets shaped by that setup instead of reading like it came from nowhere, the same way we taught it who we are.

It sources real, current trends. Rather than generating from a static prompt, it pulls from Reddit, Medium, Google, and LinkedIn itself, so what gets drafted connects to conversations actually happening, not a stale topic bank.

It generates in batches, not one post at a time. Set a cadence anywhere from three to twenty one posts a week, spaced across the days and times that make sense for your audience, and build a month of content in a single sitting instead of returning to a blank page every morning.

It includes a review step before anything publishes. Nothing goes live unchecked. You see the batch, adjust anything that misses the mark, and approve before it reaches your actual audience.

It tracks real usage. A dashboard shows posts generated, batches run, and AI operations used, connected directly to your LinkedIn account, the same dashboard we look at ourselves every week.

What it does not do

Veyrais drafts and schedules. It does not decide your positioning, build genuine relationships in your comment section, or replace the judgment of actually reading what you are about to publish before it goes out. The review step exists because a tool that publishes without a check eventually posts something that misses the moment entirely, and that costs more trust than the time it saved. Treat it as a serious head start on the mechanical half of consistency, not a replacement for having something worth saying.

How Veyrais compares to the rest of the market

Most AI LinkedIn tools fall into one of two camps. Scheduling tools, Buffer, Hootsuite, PostEverywhere, are safe and officially supported by LinkedIn's own API, but they do not generate content, you still have to write it. Content generation tools, Taplio, MagicPost, Jasper, generate posts but often charge a steep premium for that specific capability, sometimes twenty to sixty times what the underlying AI generation actually costs the provider, based on published cost breakdowns comparing subscription pricing against direct API pricing.

Veyrais sits closer to the second camp, drafting and generation, but priced without that same markup, and built by a team that uses it daily rather than a team selling a tool it does not personally rely on for its own growth. That is not a claim we expect you to take on faith, the Veyrais case study shows the actual dashboard and settings as they run in production, not a mockup built for a sales page.

Getting started

Setup follows the same shape as the tool itself, direct and without unnecessary steps. Create an account, set your background, audience, and niches, choose a tone style, connect your LinkedIn account, and generate your first batch. The review step means your first post only goes live once you have actually looked at it, which is worth doing carefully the first few times until you trust how well it has learned your voice.

Ready to see what a month of consistent posting actually takes off your plate? Start with the Starter plan and see how the drafts sound before upgrading.

Frequently asked questions

Is Veyrais the same tool Innovaria Tech uses for its own LinkedIn presence?

Yes, exactly the same one. Every post published from Innovaria Tech's own LinkedIn account runs through Veyrais, and the case study page shows the real dashboard as it runs today, not a separate demo version.

How is Veyrais different from Taplio or MagicPost?

The core capabilities overlap, AI drafted posts, trend sourcing, scheduling, but Veyrais is priced meaningfully lower at every tier compared to most established competitors, and it was built and proven on a real, ongoing LinkedIn presence before being offered to anyone else.

Does Veyrais publish automatically without me reviewing anything?

No. A review step sits before every batch goes live. You see what was generated and approve it before it reaches your actual audience, nothing publishes unchecked.

What does each pricing tier actually include?

All three tiers, Starter at fifteen dollars a month, Growth at twenty five, and Ultimate at forty, include the full dashboard, image generation, and the ability to convert posts into carousels. The difference is volume: Starter allows 30 posts and 4 batch generations a month, Growth allows 100 posts and 12 batch generations, and Ultimate allows 300 posts and 30 batch generations, with image and carousel generation limits scaling alongside each tier. Check the live pricing page for the most current numbers, since limits can be adjusted over time.

Can I cancel or change plans easily?

Plan and billing details are handled directly on the Veyrais platform. Check the current terms on the signup page for the specifics of cancellation and plan changes.

Is this the same as the custom automation systems Innovaria Tech builds for clients?

No. Veyrais is a standalone product for managing your own LinkedIn presence. Custom automation, lead response, voice agents, workflow automation, is a separate service built specifically around your business, starting with a free audit.
