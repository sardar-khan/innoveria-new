Problems we solve

Your inbox is a queue nobody manages

Email is where enquiries arrive, quotes stall, and follow ups die. Not because your team is careless, but because a shared inbox has no memory.

Enquiries sit unread overnight

A message sent at 6 pm waits 15 hours for a human. The sender does not know you are closed. They only know you are silent, and silence sends them to the next result on Google.

Opening times, pricing questions, document requests, booking changes. Your team types near identical answers dozens of times a week, and every one interrupts real work.

Everyone sends the first email. Some send the second. Almost nobody sends the third, and that is often the one that gets the answer. Deals close in the follow ups your team is too busy to write.

Questions

Email automation services, answered directly

What are email automation services?

Email automation services build systems that triage your inbox, send instant acknowledgements, answer routine questions in your voice, and run multi touch follow up sequences over email and SMS. Your team starts the day with a short digest of the messages that genuinely need a human instead of a full inbox.

How is this different from newsletter tools like Mailchimp?

Newsletter tools broadcast one message to a whole list. We build operational email: systems that respond to individual enquiries, chase quotes and documents, and stop the moment someone replies. It is response and follow up automation for your day to day inbox, not broadcast marketing.

Will automated replies sound like us?

Yes. We train the system on your past replies and approved templates, so tone, sign off, and level of detail match how your team already writes. Anything unusual or sensitive gets drafted for human review instead of being sent, so the voice never slips.

How much do email automation services cost?

A complete email automation system usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. A single workflow, such as instant acknowledgements on one inbox, starts lower. We quote one fixed price after a free audit, so there are no open ended bills.

Do sequences stop when someone replies?

Immediately. A reply, a booking, or a manual note in your CRM halts the sequence for that contact on every channel. Nobody receives a third chaser after they already answered, which is exactly the mistake manual follow up makes.

How long does setup take?

One to two weeks for a single workflow such as inbox triage or acknowledgements. Two to four weeks for a complete system with sequences, SMS, and a daily digest, including testing against your real email before you pay the final invoice.
