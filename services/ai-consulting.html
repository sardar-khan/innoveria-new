Questions

AI consulting services, answered directly

What do AI consulting services include?

AI consulting services answer the question every leadership team is being asked: what should we actually automate? We map your workflows end to end, measure where hours and revenue leak, rank every AI opportunity by payback, and hand you a fixed price roadmap. You can then build with us, with your own team, or with another vendor.

How is this different from your free audit?

The free audit is a 30 minute call that produces a written roadmap of your two or three fastest wins. Paid consulting is a deeper engagement: we map your whole operation across teams, interview the people doing the work, quantify each opportunity, and deliver a prioritized plan for the next year, not just the first month.

What deliverables do we receive?

A workflow map of how work actually moves through your business, an opportunity list with the estimated hours and revenue impact of each automation, a payback ranked roadmap with a fixed build price against every item, and tool recommendations with reasoning. Everything arrives in writing, in plain language your whole team can read.

How do you charge for consulting?

One fixed price for the engagement, quoted up front after the free audit call and scaled to the size of your operation. No day rates, no open ended retainers, no discovery phase that quietly doubles. You know the full cost before we start.

Do you implement the roadmap too?

If you want us to. Many clients have us build the top items, since a single workflow takes one to two weeks and a complete system two to four weeks, each at a fixed price with 30 days of support. But the roadmap is written so any competent operator can execute it. Hiring us twice is optional by design.

Are you tied to specific vendors or tools?

No. We take no reseller commissions and sell no software of our own, so the recommendation is whatever pays back fastest for you, whether that is n8n, Make, Zapier, an AI voice platform, or a feature already inside a tool you pay for. Everything we recommend runs in accounts you own, so there is no lock in to us either.
