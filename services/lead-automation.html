Problems we solve

Why good leads buy from someone else

You are probably generating enough leads already. The losses happen in the minutes and days after each enquiry arrives.

Leads go cold in minutes

A lead who enquires with you is enquiring with three competitors in the same sitting. The first business to reply usually wins the conversation, and a reply that takes hours might as well not arrive.

Enquiries scatter everywhere

One lead arrives through a portal, the next through your website form, the next in an Instagram DM. Nobody watches every inbox, so nobody sees the full picture, and some enquiries are never seen at all.

Follow up depends on memory

The second and third touches are where deals close, but they live in someone's head. A busy week arrives, the sticky note disappears, and a warm lead quietly becomes a lost one.

Questions

Lead automation services, answered directly

What are lead automation services?

Lead automation services build systems that capture every enquiry, reply within 60 seconds at any hour, ask your qualification questions, route each lead to the right person, and keep following up until someone books or replies. You set the rules once and the system applies them to every lead, every time.

How fast is the first reply?

Under 60 seconds, around the clock. The reply is personalized with the lead's name, what they asked about, and a clear next step, so it reads like a fast human rather than an autoresponder. Weekend and 2 am enquiries get the same speed as Tuesday morning ones.

Does lead automation work with our CRM?

In almost every case, yes. We build on HubSpot, Pipedrive, Salesforce, GoHighLevel, and most industry CRMs. If your CRM has an API, we can connect it. Every reply, qualification answer, and handoff gets logged against the contact automatically, so your pipeline stays the single source of truth.

How much do lead automation services cost?

A complete lead automation system usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. A single workflow, such as instant replies on one channel, starts lower. We quote one fixed price after a free audit, so there are no hourly meters or surprise invoices.

Does automation feel robotic to leads?

No. Every message is written in your voice, uses the lead's own details, and hands off to a human the moment a real conversation starts. Most leads never realize the first reply was automated. They just notice you answered faster than everyone else they contacted.

What channels does lead automation cover?

Website forms, listing portal enquiries, email, WhatsApp, Facebook and Instagram messages, SMS, and missed calls. If a lead can reach you on a channel, we can capture the enquiry there and reply on the same channel within 60 seconds.
