Questions

Workflow automation services, answered directly

What are workflow automation services?

Workflow automation services design and build the connections between your business tools so data moves itself: a new deal in the CRM creates the invoice, books the kickoff call, and updates the spreadsheet without anyone retyping anything. You describe how work should flow, we build it on n8n, Make, or Zapier, and you own the result.

n8n vs Make vs Zapier: which do I need?

It depends on complexity, volume, and budget. n8n suits complex logic and high volume, and self hosting it keeps platform fees near zero. Make is the visual middle ground with strong value for moderately complex work. Zapier fits tiny teams that need a few simple handoffs quickly. We recommend after the audit, and none of them pays us a commission.

What happens if an automation breaks?

You get an error alert the moment a step fails, so nothing fails silently. Every build ships with documentation for each workflow and 30 days of included support after handover. Because everything runs in accounts you own, any competent operator can maintain the system long after the project ends.

How much do workflow automation services cost?

A single workflow typically starts in the low hundreds of dollars, and a complete system connecting several tools usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. On self hosted n8n the monthly platform cost drops to near zero. We quote one fixed price after a free audit.

Do we need new software?

Almost never. Workflow automation exists to connect the CRM, calendar, invoicing, phone system, and spreadsheets you already use. If a tool has an API, we can connect it. Replacing software is the last resort, not the starting point.

Who owns the workflows after the project?

You do. Every workflow runs in accounts registered to your business, and every build ships with plain language documentation. If you stop working with us tomorrow, everything keeps running and your next operator can pick it up from the docs.
