Document chasing eats the month

Onboarding takes weeks of ping pong

Engagement letter out, signed copy back, portal invite, checklist, missing IDs, another email. A new client who was excited to switch spends their first three weeks in your inbox.

What do you charge, when is my filing due, did you get my documents, when will my return be ready. The same ten questions arrive hundreds of times, right when capacity matters most.

The system we build for you

The admin runs itself, your team reviews and signs off

One connected system that collects documents, onboards clients, answers routine questions, and drafts ledger entries, with your people approving every posting.

Document collection that never gives up politely

Each client gets a clear list of what is outstanding and a simple upload link. Reminders in your tone go out on a sensible rhythm, name the exact missing items, and stop the second the upload lands. Your close stops waiting on your inbox.

Onboarding workflows that run themselves

A signed engagement letter triggers the whole sequence: portal setup, welcome pack, document checklist, ledger access requests, and internal task creation. The client feels looked after and nobody on your team typed a thing.

An enquiry agent on duty 24/7

Fee questions, filing deadlines, document status, appointment booking: answered accurately on your website and email at any hour, from your own price list and calendar. Anything unusual routes straight to a human with full context.

Data extraction with human review

Receipts, invoices, and bank statements get read automatically and land in QuickBooks or Xero as draft entries coded to your chart of accounts. A bookkeeper approves before anything posts, so speed never costs you accuracy.

Questions firms ask

Accounting automation, answered directly

What are AI agents for finance and accounting?

Software assistants that handle the repetitive client work around your practice: collecting documents with polite persistent reminders, running onboarding checklists, answering fee and deadline questions 24/7, and extracting data from receipts and statements into your ledger. Your team reviews everything before it posts, so the agents do the chasing and the typing while your accountants do the accounting.

Is client financial data safe with automation?

Every workflow runs inside accounts your firm controls, not ours. Documents move over encrypted connections, access follows the same role based controls you set today, and nothing posts to the books without a human on your team approving it. We never take custody of client data, and you can revoke our access the day the build is done.

Can it chase clients for documents without being rude?

Yes, and that is the point. The reminders use your wording and your tone, space themselves out sensibly, reference exactly which documents are still missing, and stop the moment the client uploads. Persistent beats pushy: clients get a clear list and an easy upload link instead of a frustrated email from a partner in week three.

What does accounting automation cost?

A complete system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees. Most firms cover that with the admin hours saved in the first month end close. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your client workflows, and there is no hourly billing.

Does it work with QuickBooks and Xero?

Yes. Both have solid APIs, so extracted data from receipts, invoices, and bank statements lands in the right accounts as draft entries for your review. We build on the ledger you already run rather than replacing it, and the same applies to your practice management and portal tools if they expose an API or exports.
