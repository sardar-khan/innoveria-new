The leaks we see in every firm

Where retainers quietly disappear

Calls missed during court hours, intake details collected across three separate phone calls, and website enquiries that wait days for a reply. Every one of them is automatable.

The call that rings during court

People with urgent legal problems do not leave voicemails and wait. The caller you miss at 11 am is signing an engagement letter with the next firm by the afternoon.

Intake spread across three calls

The facts arrive in fragments: one call for the story, another for the dates, a third for the documents. Every callback burns billable hours and tests the client's patience before you are even retained.

Website enquiries wait days

Every call answered, in court or out

Questions attorneys ask

Legal intake automation, answered directly

What is an AI agent for legal?

An AI agent for legal is software that handles client intake and scheduling: it answers every call, captures case details in one structured conversation, screens for conflicts and fit against your criteria, and books qualified consultations into attorney calendars. It works around your court hours, and anything sensitive routes to a human at your firm.

Will it give legal advice?

No. The agent is strictly limited to intake and scheduling. It gathers facts, checks fit against criteria your firm defines, and books consultations. Any question that edges toward advice gets a clear response that an attorney will address it, and the conversation routes to your team. Those boundaries are built in and tested before go live.

How is client confidentiality protected?

Every intake conversation lives in accounts your firm controls, not ours. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, access is restricted to the people you name, and anything sensitive gets human review before it moves anywhere. You review the full vendor chain and every workflow before the system takes its first call.

What does an AI agent for legal cost?

A complete intake system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees, depending on call volume and integrations. Set that against the value of one retained client and the payback math is short. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your intake flow.

Does it integrate with Clio or our practice management system?

Yes. New matters, contact records, and consultation bookings land in Clio or the practice management system you already run, with the intake summary attached. If your platform has an API we connect it directly; nothing gets retyped and nothing lives in a spreadsheet on the side.
