About

We build automation you own outright

Innovaria Tech exists because most agencies sell dependence. We sell working systems, documentation, and the keys.

Why Innovaria

Innovaria Tech Limited is a software and automation company. We build the unglamorous systems that decide whether a business grows: the reply that goes out in 60 seconds, the call that gets answered at 9 pm, the follow up that happens on day 12 without anyone remembering to send it.

We work from industry playbooks: real estate, healthcare, ecommerce, legal, finance and accounting, education, manufacturing, and logistics. Because we keep solving the same industry problems, we arrive at your audit already knowing where the leaks usually are, and our builds start from proven patterns instead of blank pages. See all of them on the industries page.

The no vendor lock in promise

This is the part of our pitch clients quote back to us. Every automation we build runs in accounts registered to your business. Every workflow ships with documentation a competent operator can follow. If you leave us the day after handover, everything keeps running.

We work this way because lock in makes agencies lazy. If you can leave at any time, we have to keep earning the relationship with results, not with hostage infrastructure.

None of that means much unless you can check it, so we published the questions we think every buyer should ask: how to choose an AI automation agency. Ask us all seven, and ask the same of anyone else you are considering.

How we work

  • Fixed scope, fixed price. You approve one number before we build. No hourly meters, no surprise invoices.
  • Outcomes over tools. We quote in hours saved and leads recovered. The tech stack is our problem.
  • Weeks, not quarters. A single workflow ships in one to two weeks. Full systems in two to four.
  • Proof before final payment. You watch the automation work on your real data before you settle the invoice.
  • We use what we sell. Our own lead response, follow up, and social presence run on the same systems we build for clients. Message us and watch.

The stack we trust

We build on n8n, Make, and Zapier for workflows, plus leading AI voice and chat platforms for calls and messaging. We choose per project based on your budget and your existing tools. Where a platform fee matters, n8n can run self hosted so your monthly cost stays near zero.

What we've built

Veyrais: our own automation, running in production

We had the same bottleneck our clients have: consistent LinkedIn posting always lost to urgent work. So we ran our own audit process on ourselves and built Veyrais, an AI ghostwriter that writes in our voice, schedules a month of content in bulk, and publishes on autopilot.

It has posted for us every day since. Unlike most agency claims, this one is verifiable: ask for a live walkthrough on your audit call.
