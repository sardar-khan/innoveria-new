Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Innovaria Tech collects when you use our website or request an automation audit, how we use it, and the choices you have. We keep it plain and we keep it short.

Who we are

Innovaria Tech ("Innovaria Tech", "we", "us", "our") operates the website at innovariatech.space and provides AI and automation services. If you have any question about this policy or your data, contact us at info@innovariatech.space.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us and a small amount of standard technical data.

Information you give us

When you fill in our audit or contact form, or book a call, you may provide your name, work email, company name, phone number, and a description of your business bottleneck. When you book through our scheduling tool (Calendly), you provide the details required to confirm the meeting.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we collect basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device, referring page, and the pages you view. This is used to keep the site working, understand traffic, and improve the experience.

Cookies and analytics

We use cookies and similar technologies for essential site functions and, where enabled, for analytics (for example Google Analytics) and advertising measurement (for example Google Ads conversion tracking). These help us understand how visitors find and use the site. You can control cookies through your browser settings, and disabling them may affect some features.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to respond to your enquiry and deliver your automation audit; to schedule, confirm, and follow up on meetings; to provide and improve our services; to send you information you have requested or that is relevant to a service you are receiving; to measure the performance of our website and advertising; and to meet our legal and record keeping obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers who help us operate, and only as needed. These may include scheduling tools (Calendly), email and communication providers, hosting and analytics services, and advertising platforms such as Google. These providers process data on our behalf under their own terms and safeguards. We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect our rights.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, to maintain our business records, and to comply with legal requirements. When it is no longer needed, we delete or anonymise it.

How we protect it

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorised access. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to safeguard the data you share with us.

Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, email us at info@innovariatech.space and we will respond within a reasonable time. You can also unsubscribe from any marketing email using the link in that email.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to other websites and tools we do not control. This policy does not cover their practices, so please review their privacy policies separately.

Children

Our services are intended for businesses and are not directed to anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will publish the revised version on this page and, where appropriate, notify you. Continued use of the site after an update means you accept the revised policy.
