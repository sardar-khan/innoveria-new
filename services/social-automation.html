Questions

Social media automation services, answered directly

What are social media automation services?

Social media automation services build a posting system around your expertise: drafts generated from your ideas and material in your voice, a human approval step, scheduled publishing to LinkedIn and X, and recycling of your best content. You stay visible every day without writing every morning.

Will the posts sound like me?

Yes. The system is trained on your own posts, calls, and notes, so drafts use your vocabulary, your opinions, and your way of explaining things. You approve or edit every post before it publishes, so nothing goes out that you would not actually say.

Which platforms do you cover?

LinkedIn and X are the core builds, because that is where founders and service businesses win clients. The same pipeline can extend to a Facebook page or Instagram when those channels matter to your audience.

Do I approve posts before they publish?

Always. Drafts land in a review queue where you approve, edit, or reject each one in a minute or two. Nothing publishes without a human decision, and you can pause the whole pipeline whenever you want.

How much do social media automation services cost?

A complete posting system usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. That is a fraction of a ghostwriter's retainer, and you own the system. We quote one fixed price after a free audit.

Is automated posting allowed by LinkedIn?

Yes, when it is done properly. We schedule through official APIs and approved publishing tools, keep a human review step on every post, and never automate connection spam or mass messaging. That keeps your account safe and compliant with platform rules.
