Questions admissions teams ask

Education automation, answered directly

What is an AI agent for education?

An AI agent for education is software that handles the repetitive admissions and communication work around your institution: answering enrollment enquiries about programs, fees, and schedules 24/7, following up with interested families automatically, booking campus tours and counselor calls, and sending routine parent updates. Anything sensitive hands off to your staff.

Can it answer program and fee questions accurately?

Yes. The agent is trained only on your own materials: program descriptions, fee schedules, course schedules, and admissions policies. It answers from that source, links families to the right page, and says so when a question falls outside it, routing the conversation to your admissions team instead of guessing.

How is student data kept private?

All enquiry and student data lives in accounts your school controls, not ours. Access is restricted to the staff you name, and anything sensitive, a safeguarding concern, a special needs discussion, a complaint, hands off to a human immediately. You review every workflow and data path before the system goes live.

What does an AI agent for education cost?

A complete enquiry and follow up system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees, depending on channels and volume. A handful of additional enrollments usually covers the build. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your enquiry flow.

Does it work for tutoring centers and course creators too?

Yes. The same system fits any organization that sells learning: tutoring centers fielding parent enquiries, language schools managing trial lessons, and course creators answering pre sale questions at any hour. If enquiries arrive faster than you can answer them, the build is the same shape.
