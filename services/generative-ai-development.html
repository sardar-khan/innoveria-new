Questions

Generative AI development services, answered directly

What are generative AI development services?

Generative AI development services build custom AI into your existing workflows so it does real work: drafting customer replies, generating documents and content, summarizing calls and meetings, and pulling structured data out of messy emails and PDFs. The AI is grounded on your own documents and your team reviews its output where it matters.

How do you control accuracy and hallucinations?

Three ways. First, retrieval: the AI answers from your actual documents rather than its general memory, so output quotes your prices, policies, and facts. Second, review steps: anything customer facing or high stakes waits for a human approval before it goes out. Third, testing: we run the system against your real historical inputs before launch and measure where it gets things wrong.

What happens to our data?

It stays yours. Every system runs in accounts registered to your business, your documents live in your own storage, and we use API configurations where providers do not train on your data. Nothing about your customers or operations feeds anyone else's model.

How much do generative AI development services cost?

A single workflow such as drafted email replies typically starts in the low hundreds of dollars, and a complete system such as document generation with retrieval over your knowledge base usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. We quote a fixed price after a free audit.

How long does a generative AI project take?

One to two weeks for a single workflow, two to four weeks for a complete system including retrieval over your documents and testing against your real historical data. You see it working on your actual inputs before you pay the final invoice.

Should we build custom or buy an off the shelf AI tool?

Buy when a tool matches your exact workflow and data. Build when your process, documents, or integrations are specific to you, which is most of the time once AI needs to touch your CRM, your templates, and your tone. Custom builds here are weeks, not months, and you own the result instead of renting another subscription.
