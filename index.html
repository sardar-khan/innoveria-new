The problem

Slow follow up quietly drains your revenue every single day

Most businesses lose deals they never see. The enquiry arrived at 9 pm, the voicemail went unheard, the third follow up never got sent. None of it shows up in a report, but all of it shows up in your bank account.

Leads go cold in minutes

The odds of reaching a new lead drop sharply after the first five minutes. If your reply takes hours, the buyer has already talked to someone faster.

Missed calls become lost clients

Every unanswered call during viewings, consultations, or peak season is a client choosing whoever picks up next. Most callers never leave a voicemail. They just move on.

Your team drowns in admin

Data entry, appointment reminders, status updates, copy and paste between tools. Skilled people spend hours on work a machine should do overnight.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What is a free automation audit?

A 30 minute call where we map how leads, calls, and admin work flow through your business, find the tasks costing you the most time and revenue, and give you a written automation roadmap. The roadmap is yours to keep whether you hire us or not.

How much does business automation cost?

Most single workflow builds start in the low hundreds of dollars, and a full lead response system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 depending on how many tools we connect. Every project starts with a fixed quote after your free audit, so you never get an open ended bill.

Which tools do you build on?

We build on n8n, Make, and Zapier for workflows, plus AI voice and chat platforms for calls and messaging. We pick the stack that fits your budget and your existing CRM, calendar, and phone system. You own every account and every workflow we build.

How long does an automation build take?

A single workflow usually ships within one to two weeks. A complete lead response or intake system takes two to four weeks including testing with your real data. You see working automation before you pay the final invoice.

Do we own the systems you build?

Yes. Everything runs in accounts registered to your business, and we hand over documentation for every workflow. There is no vendor lock in: if you leave us tomorrow, everything keeps running and any competent operator can maintain it.
