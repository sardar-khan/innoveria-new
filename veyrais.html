Why it exists

The problem was ours before it was yours

Consistent LinkedIn presence brings an agency clients, but writing a post every morning does not survive contact with a busy week. We had the same bottleneck our clients have: important, repetitive work that always loses to urgent work.

So we treated ourselves as the client. We audited the workflow, found the leak, and built the system: an AI ghostwriter personalized to our voice and niches, trend sourcing from Reddit, Medium, Google, and LinkedIn, batch generation with a scheduling engine, and automatic publishing with a review step. The same audit, blueprint, build, handover process we run for every client project.

It has been posting for us ever since. Every post you see from us on LinkedIn went through this system. For the full breakdown of how it works, what each plan costs, and how it compares to other tools, see our guide to how Veyrais works.
