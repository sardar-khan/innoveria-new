Questions

AI chatbot development services, answered directly

What are AI chatbot development services?

AI chatbot development services design, train, and launch chatbots that hold real conversations with your customers on your website and WhatsApp. The bot learns from your actual pages, policies, and documents, so it answers the questions your visitors genuinely ask, qualifies them, and books calls without anyone on your team touching a keyboard.

How much does an AI chatbot cost to build?

A complete chatbot trained on your content usually lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build, plus $50 to $300 a month in platform fees. A simpler single channel bot starts lower. We quote one fixed price after a free audit, so you approve the number before any work begins.

How is the chatbot trained on our content?

We collect your website pages, FAQs, policy documents, product details, and past customer questions, then build a knowledge base the bot retrieves answers from. It responds from your approved content instead of improvising, and you review the full training set with us before launch.

Can the chatbot run on WhatsApp as well as our website?

Yes. We build on the WhatsApp Business platform, so the same bot that answers your website visitors also replies to WhatsApp messages, sends booking confirmations, and follows up after the conversation. One knowledge base, both channels, consistent answers.

What happens when a customer needs a human?

The bot hands over. It recognizes when a question sits outside its knowledge base or when a customer asks for a person, then passes the full conversation to your team with a summary so nobody repeats themselves. Outside business hours it books a call or takes a message instead.

Will the chatbot say something wrong to a customer?

We build guardrails against it. The bot answers only from the training content you approved, declines topics you mark off limits, and escalates anything uncertain to a human. Before launch we test it against real customer questions with you, and every conversation is logged so you can audit exactly what it said.
