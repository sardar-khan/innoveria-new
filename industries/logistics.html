AI agents for logistics operations that answer where is my shipment for you

AI agents answer shipment status questions on phone, email, and WhatsApp from live TMS data, send delay notifications before customers notice, and chase carriers for check ins and PODs, so dispatch runs freight instead of relaying it. Built around the TMS you already run.

The drag we see in every operation

Where dispatch hours quietly disappear

Status calls your TMS could answer, dispatchers relaying the same updates, and carrier check calls eating the morning. All three are automatable.

The phone rings with questions your TMS can answer

Where is my delivery, has it left the dock, what is the ETA. The answer sits in your TMS the whole time, but a person still has to pick up, look it up, and read it out. All day.

Dispatch spends hours relaying the same updates

A load slips, and a dispatcher tells the customer, the receiver, and the account manager the same thing three times. Multiply by every delayed load and the day is gone before the freight moves.

Carrier check calls eat the morning

Did the driver pick up, where is the truck now, will it make the appointment. Chasing carriers for check ins and PODs is necessary work that does not need a human dialing the numbers.

The system we build for you

Customers stay informed, dispatch stays on the freight

One connected system that answers status questions from live data, notifies before customers ask, and runs the carrier chasing on its own.

A tracking agent on phone, email, and WhatsApp

Customers ask where their shipment is and get the current status and ETA from live TMS data, on whichever channel they used, at any hour. Anything beyond a status lookup routes to a dispatcher with the load details attached.

Proactive delay and delivery notifications

When an ETA slips past your threshold, the customer hears it from you first, with the new time. When a load delivers, the confirmation and POD go out automatically. Customers who already know do not call.

Automated carrier check in and POD collection

Carriers get check in requests at pickup, in transit, and at delivery, and PODs get requested, received, and filed against the load without a dispatcher dialing. Gaps and no responses escalate to a human.

Dock appointment scheduling

Carriers book and reschedule dock appointments against your live availability instead of trading voicemails with the warehouse. Detention arguments get shorter when the timestamps are clean.

Questions operators ask

Logistics automation, answered directly

What are AI agents for logistics operations?

Software assistants that handle the communication load around your freight: answering where is my shipment questions on phone, email, and WhatsApp from live TMS data, sending proactive delay and delivery notifications, checking in with carriers, collecting PODs, and scheduling dock appointments. Dispatch keeps the judgment calls; the agents do the relaying.

Can it read from our TMS?

If your TMS has an API or can produce exports, yes. The tracking agent answers from the same live statuses your dispatchers see, so customers get the truth, not a script. We build around the platform you already run and never ask you to replace it. Tracking spreadsheets work as a starting point too.

Does it handle phone calls or just email?

Phone, email, and WhatsApp. Most where is my shipment questions arrive by phone, so the voice agent answers on the first ring, looks up the load, and gives the current status and ETA. Anything unusual, like a claim or an angry customer, routes straight to a dispatcher with the full context attached.

What does logistics automation cost?

A complete system typically lands between $1,000 and $5,000 to build plus $50 to $300 in monthly platform fees. Most operations cover that with the dispatcher hours saved on status calls alone. You get a fixed quote after a free audit of your communication flow, and there is no hourly billing.

How do proactive delay notifications work?

The system watches shipment statuses and ETAs in your TMS. When a load slips past a threshold you set, the customer gets an update with the new ETA before they notice the delay, on the channel they prefer. Delivered loads trigger confirmations and POD delivery the same way. Customers who already know do not call.
